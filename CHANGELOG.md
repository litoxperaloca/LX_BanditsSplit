# Changelog

All notable changes to **LX Bandits Split-Screen Fix**.

## v1.0.0

Update to an already published mod. Earlier versions are on the Workshop page.

### Changed in this version

- Russian translation added (EN/ES/AR/RU).

### Included

- Damage goes to the right player
- Players stop being indistinguishable
- Visibility uses your own light
- Companions follow who recruited them
- Raids and ambient AI spread out
- Bandits investigate noise from anyone *(optional)*
