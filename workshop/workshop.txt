description=[h1]LX Bandits Split-Screen Fix[/h1]
description=Splitscreen / split screen / local couch co-op fix for Project Zomboid Build 42 (B42). Makes Bandits target the right player when 2, 3 or 4 of you share one screen, on keyboard or gamepad.
description=In split-screen every bandit action landed on player 1, whoever it was aimed at. Player 2 was a ghost that took no hits and dealt no consequences.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Damage goes to the right player[/b] - Bandits detect, chase, hit, shoot and shove whoever they are actually engaging, instead of routing everything through player 1.
description=[*][b]Players stop being indistinguishable[/b] - Outside online multiplayer, Bandits gave every player the same character ID - literally the number 1.
description=[*][b]Visibility uses your own light[/b] - How visible a character is was always computed with player 1's light level, which includes player 1's vision cone.
description=[*][b]Companions follow who recruited them[/b] - A recruited companion followed player 1 no matter who recruited them.
description=[*][b]Raids and ambient AI spread out[/b] - Raids spawn around any player. Bandit chatter, endurance and task sounds use the nearest player instead of player 1.
description=[*][b]Bandits investigate noise from anyone *(optional)*[/b] - When any local player makes a loud noise - a gunshot, an alarm, banging on a door - hostile bandits that heard it remember the…
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3268487204]Bandits[/url]
description=[b]Load it AFTER[/b] Bandits2.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
description=[b]Options:[/b] Options → MODS → [b]LX Bandits Split-Screen Fix[/b]. The split-screen fixes are always on; the options only control the optional noise-investigation AI and its minimum noise radius.
